Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Be Extrovert ("we", "us", "our") provides the Be Extrovert mobile app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Who can use the app

Be Extrovert is for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Information we collect

Information you provide:

  • Email address and username
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form)
  • Timezone
  • Challenge completion history, ratings and notes you write about challenges
  • Progress and statistics (levels, streaks, achievements)
  • Messages you send through "Report a Problem", including any reply email you choose to give

Information collected automatically:

  • When you report a problem, the app attaches the app version and your device platform and OS version.
  • The app stores your sign-in tokens on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which can collect device identifiers, the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location. See "Advertising" below.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in
  • To save and show your challenge progress, levels and statistics
  • To send password reset emails and other account emails you request
  • To read and respond to problem reports
  • To show ads and, where allowed, personalized ads
  • To keep the app secure, including limiting repeated sign-in and reset attempts

Who we share information with

  • Brevo: our email provider. It receives the recipient email address and the content of account emails, such as password reset emails and problem reports we forward to our support inbox.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads in the app. It processes the advertising data described above under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Hosting provider: our server and database are hosted on Render.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers above, or when required by law.

Advertising and consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice later from the app's privacy options where available.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information and progress while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your email, username, challenge history, ratings, notes and progress statistics right away. This deletion cannot be undone. Email delivery logs kept by our email provider are handled under that provider's own retention rules.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: open the Profile tab and tap Delete Account. If you cannot use the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email us (see Contact below).

Security

We protect your information with measures such as hashed passwords, tokens that expire, reset links that expire after one hour and can be used only once, and limits on repeated sign-in and reset attempts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct your account details in the app
  • Delete your account from the Profile tab
  • Ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Withdraw advertising consent through the consent options in the app, where available

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, such as the GDPR or the CCPA. To use them, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above when we do. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
