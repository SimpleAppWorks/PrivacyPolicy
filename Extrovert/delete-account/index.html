Delete Your Account

Be Extrovert, operated by Simple App Works Ltd. Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can permanently delete your Be Extrovert account and the personal data linked to it. Deletion cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Be Extrovert and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Account Actions and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in or open the app, email us from the address registered to your account:

  1. Send an email to simpleappworksltd@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my Be Extrovert account.
  3. Include the username of the account. Do not include your password.

We may reply to confirm the request before deleting, to make sure it comes from the account owner. We will complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving your request.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and username
  • Your password (stored as a one-way hash)
  • Your timezone setting
  • Your challenge history, ratings and notes
  • Your progress, levels, streaks and statistics

What may be kept

  • Emails we sent to you, which our email provider (Brevo) keeps under its own retention rules.
  • Problem reports you sent us, which we keep in our support inbox only as long as needed to respond. You can ask us to delete them too.
  • Ads and advertising data processed by Google AdMob, which are governed by Google's privacy policy.
Questions about deletion? Read our full Privacy Policy or email simpleappworksltd@gmail.com.
