Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Thin Launcher ("we", "us", "our") is a minimal home-screen launcher app for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Who can use the app

Thin Launcher does not require sign-up or an account. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Information we collect

Information you provide: none. There is no account, profile, or sign-in anywhere in the app.

Information collected automatically:

  • The list of apps installed on your device, read using Android's QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. This is used only on your device, to let you pick which apps appear on your home screen ("Focus Apps"). It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which can collect device identifiers, the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to show ads and, where permitted, personalized ads. See "Advertising" below.
  • If you buy Ad-Free, Pro, or send a tip, the purchase is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment card details — we only receive the product ID and purchase status, used to unlock the feature you paid for.

How we use this information

  • To let you choose which installed apps show on your home screen
  • To show ads and, where allowed, personalized ads
  • To unlock Ad-Free or Pro features after a purchase, and to restore them if you reinstall the app

Who we share information with

  • Google AdMob: shows ads in the app and processes the advertising data described above under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: process your purchase. We do not receive or store your payment details.

We do not sell your personal information. Thin Launcher has no backend server and no analytics SDK — we do not otherwise collect, store, or share your data.

Advertising and consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. Buying Ad-Free or Pro removes ads entirely.

Data stored on your device

Your Focus Apps selection, colour theme, search preference, and a local record of what you've purchased are stored only on your device (not on any server of ours, and not tied to an account). We have no way to access, change, or delete this data remotely. Uninstalling the app removes it.

App permissions

Depending on your device, the installed app may request:

  • Query all apps: to list your installed apps so you can pick your Focus Apps (used on-device only, as above).
  • Internet: required to load ads and verify purchases.
  • Vibrate: for tap feedback.
  • A small number of other permissions (such as display-over-other-apps, activity recognition, or storage access) are included by the ad or billing libraries the app uses, and are not actively used by any Thin Launcher feature.

Security

Because Thin Launcher has no account and no server, there is no central store of your personal data for us to protect or that could be breached. Data on your device is protected by your device's own operating system and storage security.

Your choices and rights

  • Withdraw advertising consent through the consent options in the app, where available
  • Revoke any device permission at any time from your system settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, such as the GDPR or the CCPA. To use them, contact us — though since we hold no personal data tied to you, there is typically nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on our end.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above when we do. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
