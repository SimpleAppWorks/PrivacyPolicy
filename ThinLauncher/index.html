Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Thin Launcher ("we", "us", "our") is developed and operated by Simple App Works Ltd, with Vathirajan.v as the app's developer. Thin Launcher is a minimal home-screen launcher app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Thin Launcher does not require sign-up or an account anywhere in the app. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide: none. There is no account, profile, or sign-in anywhere in the app.

Information collected automatically:

  • The list of apps installed on your device, read using Android's QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. This is used only on your device, to let you pick which apps appear on your home screen ("Focus Apps"). It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which can collect device identifiers, the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to show ads and, where permitted, personalized ads. On supported Android versions, AdMob also uses the Android Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics, Attribution Reporting, and the Privacy Sandbox advertising ID) to select ad interest topics, measure ad conversions, and prevent fraud, as a more private alternative or complement to the classic advertising ID. See Section 5 below.
  • If you buy Ad-Free, Pro, or send a tip, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment card details — we only receive the product ID and purchase status, used to unlock the feature you paid for.

3. How we use this information

  • To let you choose which installed apps show on your home screen
  • To show ads and, where allowed, personalized ads
  • To unlock Ad-Free or Pro features after a purchase, and to restore them if you reinstall the app

4. Who we share information with

  • Google AdMob: shows ads in the app and processes the advertising data described above under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play: processes your purchase. We do not receive or store your payment details.

We do not sell your personal information. Thin Launcher has no backend server and no analytics SDK — we do not otherwise collect, store, or share your data.

5. Advertising and consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob to users who have not purchased Ad-Free or Pro. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform); you can revisit this choice at any time from Settings. Buying Ad-Free or Pro removes ads entirely and stops the app from requesting further advertising data.

6. Data stored on your device

Your Focus Apps selection, colour theme, search preference, and a local record of what you've purchased are stored only on your device (not on any server of ours, and not tied to an account). We have no way to access, change, or delete this data remotely. Uninstalling the app removes it.

7. App permissions

Depending on your device, the installed app may request:

  • Query all apps: to list your installed apps so you can pick your Focus Apps (used on-device only, as above).
  • Internet: required to load ads and verify purchases.
  • Vibrate: for tap feedback.
  • Advertising ID and Android Privacy Sandbox access (AD_ID, Topics, Attribution Reporting): used by Google AdMob to show ads, measure ad performance, and, where you've consented, personalize ads.
  • A small number of other permissions (such as network-state checks, wake-lock, or foreground-service access) are included by the ad or billing libraries the app uses, and are not actively used by any Thin Launcher feature on its own.

8. Security

Because Thin Launcher has no account and no server, there is no central store of your personal data for us to protect or that could be breached. Data on your device is protected by your device's own operating system and storage security. Data sent to Google AdMob and Google Play for ads and purchases is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Children's privacy

Thin Launcher is a general-audience app and is not directed at or marketed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), in line with COPPA (US) and equivalent children's privacy laws.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke any device permission at any time from your system settings. Setting Thin Launcher as your default home launcher can be undone at any time from your device's Home app settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Manage AdMob's advertising data use through your device's ad settings (e.g. "Reset advertising ID" / "Opt out of ad personalization" on Android).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the CCPA — including the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. We do not sell your personal information. To use these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12 below — though since we hold no personal data tied to you on any server, there is typically nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on our end; requests relating to data collected directly by Google AdMob should be directed to Google, and applicable ad opt-outs can be exercised through your device's ad settings as described above.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above when we do. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
