Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Myself is developed and operated by Simple App Works Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), based in India, with Vathirajan as the app's developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information Myself collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is governed by the laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), to the extent applicable.

2. Information we collect

Myself is designed to work fully offline and does not require you to create an account. The information handled by the app is:

  • Your first name — collected once when you first open the app, so the app can greet you. Stored only on your device; never transmitted to us or any server.
  • Favorites, streak count, and reminder settings — generated as you use the app (e.g. tapping the heart icon, your daily open streak, your notification time). Stored only on your device.
  • Notification permission — if you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device via your OS. No notification content is sent through any external server.
  • Advertising data, via Google AdMob — Myself shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), approximate location, IP address, and app usage data, and may use this information to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. This collection is performed by Google, not by Myself directly. On supported Android versions, AdMob also uses the Android Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics, Attribution Reporting, and the Privacy Sandbox advertising identifier) as a more private alternative to the classic advertising ID, to select ad interest topics and measure ad performance without tracking you individually across apps. On iOS, before any cross-app tracking identifier (IDFA) is used for ad personalization, Myself requests your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework; if you decline, or have "Allow Apps to Request to Track" turned off, Myself still shows ads, but they are not personalized to you using IDFA. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Myself shows an in-app consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized advertising takes place, letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads, allow only non-personalized ads, or manage your choices per advertising partner; you can revisit this choice at any time from Settings. You can also learn more and manage your ad preferences at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Purchase information, via Google Play Billing / Apple App Store — Myself offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and optional one-time support "tips." These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not collect, see, or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). We only receive confirmation of which product was purchased, which is stored on your device to remove ads and to let you restore your purchase later. Google and Apple's own privacy policies govern the payment information they collect to process the transaction.

3. We do not have a server or accounts

Myself has no backend server and no user accounts. We — the developer — do not receive, store, or have access to your name, favorites, streak, settings, or purchase history. Everything described in Section 2 (other than data collected directly by Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, or the Apple App Store) stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Any data that does leave your device — such as ad requests to Google AdMob or purchase confirmations to Google Play / the Apple App Store — is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Because Myself has no server or account system, there is no central database of your personal data for us to protect, or that could be breached on our end.

4. How information is used

On-device data is used solely to run the app's features: greeting you by name, showing your favorited affirmations, tracking your daily streak, and sending your chosen reminder. AdMob's data is used by Google to serve and measure advertising, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

5. Children's privacy

Myself is a general-audience app and is not directed at or marketed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), in line with COPPA (US) and equivalent children's privacy laws. Myself is not listed under Google Play's Families Policy Program.

6. Your rights and choices

Because Myself stores data only on your device, you are in direct control of it at all times:

  • You can review or change your reminder settings anytime in the Settings tab.
  • You can remove your on-device data at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • You can manage AdMob's advertising data use through your device's ad settings (e.g. "Reset advertising ID" / "Opt out of ad personalization" on Android, or App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

If you are in India and wish to exercise rights available to you under the DPDP Act regarding any data processed by Myself itself (as opposed to data collected directly by Google AdMob), you may contact our Grievance Officer using the details in Section 9 below.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), such as the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell your personal information. Because Myself itself stores data only on your device and has no server, there is typically no personal data held by us for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf; requests relating to data collected directly by Google AdMob should be directed to Google, and you can exercise applicable ad opt-outs through your device's ad settings as described in Section 2 above.

7. Data retention

On-device data persists until you clear it, uninstall the app, or your device removes it. We do not retain any copy of it elsewhere, because we never receive it.
